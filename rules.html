COMMUNITY RULES

Play fair. Have fun.

Keep PringleSMP welcoming and enjoyable. Staff may take action when behavior harms the server or other players.

01 · Respect players

No harassment, targeted abuse, threats or disruptive behavior toward other members.

02 · No cheating

Do not use unauthorized clients, cheats, exploits or tools that give an unfair gameplay advantage.

03 · No exploiting bugs

Report serious bugs instead of intentionally abusing them for an advantage.

04 · Keep chat appropriate

Avoid spam, excessive flooding and content that makes the community uncomfortable.

05 · No malicious activity

Do not attempt to damage the server, steal accounts, spread malware or disrupt services.

06 · Staff decisions

Follow staff instructions during moderation situations. If you disagree with an action, use the appropriate appeal/support channel.
